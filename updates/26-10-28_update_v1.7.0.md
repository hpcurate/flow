## What was done

**The visualiser on the stage** (r261028-vv0d)
- A **Visualiser** chip on the stage, beside the timer visual's (and **Shift+V**), draws the music visualiser behind the clock
  instead of the timer visual: the same six styles as full screen, the clock over it as over full screen's.
- **Each view its own switch**: the standard view's and the minimal view's are kept apart (settings → visualiser: "On the
  stage", "In the minimal view"), so the minimal view can keep its timer visual while the standard view shows the music, or
  the other way round.
- **The minimal view, fitted:** the visualiser a size smaller there (0.82 of its scale, at most 0.6 of its density), so the
  clock keeps its room on the narrow stage. At the minimal view's narrowest, the stage's row tightens to keep the third chip.
- While it is on, the chip beside it (the timer visual's) goes through the visualiser's styles instead.
- **Decided here:** off by default in both views (the stage stays as it was until switched on).

**The artwork stylised** (r261028-ylsm)
- Settings → visualiser → **Stylise the artwork** (on by default, the switch to turn it off).
- **Spotify:** its album's artwork as a duotone: the theme's dark in the shadows, the album's own strongest colour in the
  lights. A poster in FLOW's dark, flat look, still the album's; the cover around it (its sleeve) in that colour's family too.
- **Anything else:** FLOW's own Bauhaus cover, every shape in one colour's family (the colour, a tint, a shade, a mid tone), in
  the colour of where the sound comes from:
  - **a website:** its favicon's main colour. FLOW finds the site from the browser's window titles (the window whose title has
    the track's title in it; the site's name after it, as in "(3) Archangel - YouTube - Brave"). About twenty sites are known
    with their colours: YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Twitch, Bandcamp, Mixcloud, Deezer, Apple Music, Spotify's web
    player, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Crunchyroll, Kick, Vimeo, Beatport, Audiomack, Plex, Jellyfin, Radio Garden, BBC
    Sounds;
  - **an app:** a known player's colour (Spotify, the browsers, Media Player, VLC, Deezer, Apple Music, MusicBee, AIMP,
    TIDAL…), else **the main colour of its icon** on this PC (its exe, found by its process).
  - Nothing known (an unknown site, the playing tab not in front of its window): FLOW's own cover in the theme.
- **Decided here:**
  - **nothing is fetched.** FLOW stays offline, so the sites' colours are known in `art.js` rather than read from their
    favicons online; an app's comes from its icon on the PC.
  - A window's title is read once per track, only to find the site, and never kept.

**CORE 0.27.0's timer colours, in FLOW's window** (Hugo's r261028-hr3y, CORE's)
- `ink.js` is CORE's `core/ink.js`: each timer colour's own text colour, of its hue and readable. CORE's "names in capitals"
  reaches FLOW's window too.
