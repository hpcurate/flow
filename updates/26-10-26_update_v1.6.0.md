## What was done

### What moved to CORE (`core/clock/`)
- The pill (`pill-main.js`, `pill.html/css/js`, `pill-preload.js`, `pill.ps1`), the timers (`timers-main.js`, `timers.html`,
  `timers-pop.js`, `timers-preload.js`, `ring.*`) and the TUTOR / GRIND link's pipe server. They run in CORE, which is
  always on. FLOW keeps `timers-ui.js` and `timers.css` for its own window's dock.

### FLOW now (`main.js`)
- **A relay to CORE**: FLOW connects to CORE's pipe for it (`\\.\pipe\hugo-core-flow`) and passes on what its page says to
  the pill, the timers and the link, and what they say back. The page didn't change: its timer mirrors the session, its
  music follows the breaks, the chip, the timers docked in every view, the drawer's pill and timers tabs. It looks for
  CORE again every 3 s, and when CORE comes it tells it what the page last said (its music, its colours, its intention).
- **TUTOR or GRIND started FLOW to link** (their pipe wasn't there): FLOW starts CORE, which serves their pipe now.
- **Its old arguments** (`--flow-timer=…`, `--flow-session=…`, `--flow-view=pill`) go to CORE, kept until it's there.
- **The tray** loses the pill's and the timers' items (they're in CORE's tray). `link.json` isn't written any more.

### FLOW's page (`app.js`)
- **The pomodoro, kept by CORE**: with CORE there, the start button on a fresh pomodoro starts it in CORE's clock as an
  open session (focus, break, long break every n, the goal, auto next). FLOW mirrors it like any open session. A finished
  focus block counts in today's sessions, its minutes in today's "focused", and a long break shows as one.
- **CORE away**: FLOW's own timer runs as it always did. The pill and timers switches (and `O`, `N`) say "CORE keeps the
  pill and the timers — it isn't running".
