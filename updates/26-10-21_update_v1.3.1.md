## What was done

**Measured first.** A new `npm run perf` (`test/perf.cjs`) runs FLOW on a temp
profile with a private link pipe. Other apps' media is off, and FLOW plays its
own generated loop with the bus cut off from the speakers: the visuals hear
music, the room doesn't. It reads Electron's per-process counters and FLOW's
PowerShell helpers, as % of the whole CPU (Task Manager's figure). "At rest" is
FLOW in the background; "in use" is FLOW focused.

| state (music on) | before | after, at rest | after, in use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| standard | 5.60 % | 1.12 % | 2.61 % |
| minimal | 5.09 % | 0.91 % | 2.39 % |
| full screen | 5.55 % | 2.41 % | 2.43 % |
| mini bar | 3.54 % | 0.54 % | 0.55 % |
| hidden | 4.62 % | 0.33 % | 0.33 % |
| hidden + pill | 7.50 % | 1.03 % | 1.02 % |

Before, the last row is Hugo's "up to 8%".

**What cost it**, found by turning things off one at a time:
- **The covers' turning shapes** (CSS on SVG shapes) redrew the big cover 60
  times a second: ~3 %. They kept going with FLOW hidden, because
  `backgroundThrottling: false` keeps a hidden page painting.
- **The draw loop** redrew the dial at 60 fps even with nothing changing: ~2 %.
  A frame request that returns early still costs a frame, so lowering the fps
  alone saved little.
- **The pill** (a see-through window over everything, so every redraw sends the
  whole window again): its music bars 30 times a second, each eased, and its
  progress fill eased on every 250 ms tick. ~1–3 %.
- **Progress fills** set every second with a 1 s width transition were never
  still: a relayout every frame.
- **The film grain's blend** multiplies every redraw under it (~0.7 %). It was
  kept for the look; there are far fewer redraws now.

**The changes:**
- **The draw loop's pace:**
  - With nothing on screen (hidden, minimised), it stops.
  - In the mini bar, only its little cover turns, 15 times a second while
    music plays.
  - In use (focused, or the pointer moved over FLOW in the last 8 s) and in
    full screen, it runs at the frame rate setting.
  - Otherwise it draws 20 times a second. Settings → Visualiser → **Slow down
    when you're elsewhere** (on) turns that off.
  - Below 50 fps, a timer wakes it for the next frame.
- **The covers turn from that loop**, 15 times a second while music plays, and
  hold their angle when it stops.
- **Progress fills no longer ease** (mini bar, full screen, the timers, the
  pill's bars and docked timers). They move a fraction of a pixel per step
  anyway.
- **The pill's music bars** update 20 times a second, without easing.

**Decisions made for Hugo:**
- "At rest" draws 20 fps. Full screen is exempt: you watch it.
- Covers turn 15 times a second (one turn takes 26 s, so it's ~1° per step).
- The pill's music bars: 20 updates a second, no easing.
- The grain's blend stays.
